CELEBRATION OF LIFE SOFTWARE | Funeral Notice

Quickly send funeral notices to friends and family

EverArk's celebration of life software makes it quick and easy to create a funeral notice to send to friends and family. Simply use the funeral notice form to add the service location, date, and other details you want to include.

See how Funeral Notices work.
Design Funeral Notice
Use the online form to quickly design and send service and funeral notices.
By filling in all the fields in the funeral notice form, your online invite will include name, a profile picture, a beautiful background, a tribute, date, time, and place of service(s).
- EverArk Celebration of LIfe Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Funeral Notice
Write Obituary
Write an obituary to send out with the funeral notice.
By writing and sharing an obituary, you are inviting friends and family to join in celebrating a loved one's life.  It is an opportunity to remember the love and connection shared with the deceased.  
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Funeral Notice > Obituary section
Include the Cause of Death
Announce one's death in a meaningful and lasting way.
By writing an obituary and announcing one's death, you can share with family, friends, and the public one's passing. You can further honor their life by adding a eulogy to distribute electronically and/or read at the funeral service.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Funeral Notice > Cause of Death section
Add a Prayer
Pay tribute to those who passed away by sharing a prayer.
By adding a prayer, poem, or verse to the funeral notice, you can share with friends and family who may or may not attend the services a short tribute to the deceased.  It is also a way to help those without words express their feelings.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Funeral Notice > Prayer section
Setup Charity
Setup a charity in the name of the deceased.
By setting up a charity in the name of the deceased, friends and family can donate for their favorite cause.  This is a way to honor and memorialize their loved one.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Funeral Notice > Charity link
Send Funeral Notice
Send the funeral notice to friends and family.
By electronically sending the funeral notice to family and friends, you are inviting those close to the deceased to come and participate in their celebration of life services.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Publish button
