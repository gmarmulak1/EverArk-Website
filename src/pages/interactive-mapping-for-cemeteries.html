IS INTERACTIVE MAPPING IMPORTANT FOR CEMETERIES?

Interactive mapping can be very important for cemeteries, as it can provide a range of benefits for visitors, staff, and management. Here are some reasons why interactive mapping can be useful for cemeteries:
​​

Easily Search and Find Loved Ones in a Cemetery

With interactive mapping, cemetery visitors can use a digital map to search for a specific person or location, and get directions to the gravesite or location they are looking for. This can save time and reduce frustration, particularly in large cemeteries with many burial plots and monuments.
​​

Help Cemetery Staff Manage Plot Locations

Interactive mapping can also be helpful for cemetery staff, as it allows them to easily update and manage the cemetery's records, including burial plot locations and other important information. This can help ensure that cemetery records are accurate and up-to-date, which can be important for genealogy research and other purposes.
​

Cemetery Mapping Facilitates Genealogy Research

Additionally, interactive mapping can also be useful for genealogy research. With the ability to search for gravesites and view detailed information about them, genealogists can easily locate and gather information about their ancestors buried in the cemetery. This can be particularly valuable for individuals who may not live in the area where their ancestors are buried, allowing them to conduct research from anywhere with an internet connection.

Overall, interactive mapping can provide significant benefits for cemeteries, making it easier for both visitors and staff to navigate and manage the cemetery's resources.
