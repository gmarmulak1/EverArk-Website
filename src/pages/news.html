EverArk Enters Exclusive Partnership with The Funeral Services Collective
The Collective will be the exclusive U.S. sales partner for the state-of-the-art cemetery management software
​
​Rocklin, CA – Many industries have shifted from paper to digital over the years, but the funeral industry was overdue for a technological revolution. Two weeks ago, EverArk launched its new cemetery management software at the ICCFA Annual Convention and Exhibition in Las Vegas. The software will not only streamline funeral and cemetery management but also allow friends and family to create unique online legacies to remember their loved ones after they’re gone.

“We’re excited to work closely with the Collective, especially in the wake of our launch last month at ICCFA,” said Greg Marmulak, CEO of EverArk. “They’re innovators in an industry that has typically been slow to evolve, and we’re excited our software will be provide a modern solution for their customers.” 

“The Funeral Collective was impressed by EverArk’s technology which aligns with our mission to save cemetery’s overhead costs and empowering funeral directors to grow their businesses,” said Sara Schmitz, CEO of The Funeral Collective. “Through this partnership, we’ll be providing our customers with cutting-edge tools and major cost savings.” 

​EverArk allows customers to manage their business at a reasonable price point. The software provides tools to manage operations, like interactive maps, sales, customers, records, and work orders. Users can say goodbye to manually managing records, including paper maps and sales contracts. EverArk is also the first cemetery management software that provides a personalized, online link to sell plots online via a cemetery’s own website.  The Digital Legacy Packages let cemeteries profit from selling digital legacy packages alongside their plot sales and services. The Digital Legacy component allows loved ones to create a digital presence and memorial to celebrate the lives of the deceased during their time of bereavement and beyond. 

EverArk provides a 30-day free trial to all cemeteries so they can get a feeling for the software and its ease of use. Standard beta pricing is $12 per user per month for the Pro plan which allows cemeteries to manage unlimited gardens, plots, and users.  There’s also an Enterprise plan for companies managing multiple cemeteries.
