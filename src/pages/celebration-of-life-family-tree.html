CELEBRATION OF LIFE SOFTWARE | Family Tree

Easily map the family tree of your nearest relatives

EverArk's celebration of life software makes it easy to map your family tree from your grandparents to your grandchildren. Preserve your family ancestry for future generations.

See how Family Tree works.
Add Your Genealogy
Help preserve your family history for future generations.
By adding your parents, grand-parents, and great-grand parents to your genealogy, you're not only leaving behind your family history, you're teaching your future generations about the past and where they come from.
- EverArk Celebration of LIfe Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree
Add Your Descendants
Leave your children and grandchildren with a sense of belonging.
By adding your children and grandchildren to your family tree, you're including them in the tradition and history of your family and leaving them with a sense of belonging.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree
Upload Family Photos
Be reminded of your roots with profile pictures.
By uploading and adding photos to the members of your family tree, future generations can enjoy and be reminded of those that came before them, establishing a sense of connection.  
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree
Share Family Details
Help establish a lasting bond among family members.
By updating your family tree with contact details, personal demographics, birthdates, and dates of passing, you're leaving behind your legacy and relationships for your family to bond over.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree
Publish Family Tree
Publish and add your family tree to your digital legacy
By publishing your family tree so that it's included in your digital legacy, you're teaching those that come after you of your family history, important for medical reasons.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree > Publish button
Send Family Tree
Invite family and friends to view your family tree.
By publishing your family tree, you can now share and send it to family and friends.  They can see it in your digital legacy in either the web or app version of EverArk.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Family Tree > Copy Link
