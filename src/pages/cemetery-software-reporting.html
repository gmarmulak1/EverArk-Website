CEMETERY MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE | Analysis

Start managing and analyzing your cemetery with custom reports

EverArk's cemetery reporting software makes it easy to manage all aspects of your cemetery. You can create custom data reports to analyze your data or download any of the template reports. You can also use any of our dashboards to quickly gain insight to your cemetery performance.

See how Analysis works.
Build Data Reports
Build custom data reports and export to Excel for analysis.
By using EverArk's reporting software, you can create custom data lists of inventory, sales, invoices, customers, and operations.  Add or drop fields, set field order, create custom fields, set default views, and export data are some of the many features and benefits of reporting.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Reports > Inventory, Sales, Customers, Invoices, or Maintenance
Open Template Reports
Choose a template sales report to view and share.
By selecting any of our template reports, you can see and manage your sales commissions, your sales tax, your endowment fund, and your monthly sales report.  If we don't have what you're looking for, submit a request for us to build a new template for you.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Reports > Sales
View Cemetery Performance
Manage and track performance for your cemetery, gardens, and plots.
By using EverArk's cemetery management dashboards. you can track and monitor the key performance indicators for your cemetery, your gardens, and your plots.  You can drill-down into the details of each view for further analysis and understanding.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Dashboards > Cemetery, Gardens, or Plots
Review Deals
Review your sales pipeline to track the sales cycle of each deal.
By reviewing our cemetery sales dashboard, you can quickly see all your deals and where they are in your sales cycle from lead to opportunity to account.  You can easily track your sales team, the lead source, the estimated close date, and the sale amount.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Sales
Manage Invoicing
See a revenue summary of what invoices are due, late, and paid.
By using our cemetery invoice dashboard, you can see how much has been paid, how many invoices are due and late, how many days it takes for your customers to pay and track your monthly revenue.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Invoices
Track Operations
Track the performance of assigned maintenance and services.
By using our operations dashboard, you can track the performance of your employees and the state of all work orders and services.  You can see a summary of past due work orders, the number of work orders completed on time, the average time to close a work order, and more.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Maintenance
