CELEBRATION OF LIFE SOFTWARE | Resting Place

Share with loved ones your final resting place

EverArk's celebration of life software makes it easy to add the final resting place of a loved one. You add the details yourself or quickly geotag the location using the app, include an online memorial, and share it with friends and family.

See how Resting Places work.
Buy a Plot
Shop all EverArk managed plots in the EverAfterly store.
By shopping on the EverAfterly ecommerce site, you can purchase a plot from any of the cemeteries using EverArk's cemetery management software.  You can manage your purchase and payments within your celebration of life account.
- EverArk Celebration of LIfe Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Buy a Plot link
Geotag a Burial
Capture the exact GPS location of any gravesite.
By geotagging your exact location, you can capture the GPS coordinate of any gravesite to share with family and friends. You can use the coordinates to get driving and walking directions to the resting place.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Geotag a Plot link
Add Cemetery Details
Save the cemetery details to share with loved ones.
By completing the cemetery profile and adding details such as a cemetery photo, address, description, garden, plot location, geo landmarks and more, you can memorialize your burial site so your loved ones can visit.  
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Location tab
Share Resting Place
Share your final resting place with friends and family.
By sharing your final resting place with family and friends, they can save it so they can get directions to visit and proximity alerts when nearby.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Location tab
Create a 3D Avatar
Upload a photo to generate a 3D Avatar.
By uploading a photo and creating a 3D avatar of yourself, future generations will be able to enjoy a live image of you.  Your avatar will appear at your gravesite using augmented reality in the EverArk app.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Avatar tab
Request Maintenance
When you buy a plot, you can request maintenance.
By buying a plot online from one of EverArk's cemetery customers, you can make a service request to remove debris, clean headstone, trim grass, and more.  Keep the resting place of your loved one clean and untarnished.
- EverArk Celebration of Life Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Resting Place > Maintenance tab
