CELEBRATION OF LIFE SOFTWARE | Online Memorial

Publishing memorials to the web have never been easier

EverArk's celebration of life software makes it easy for you manage and publish digital memorials to the web. Everlasting memorials can be shared among family and friends so they can share stories, photos, videos, and more.

See how Online Memorials work.
Design Memorial
Use the online form to quickly design and complete your everlasting memorial.
By filling in all the fields in the profile form, your online memorial will include name and dates, a profile picture, a beautiful background, a quote, a tribute, and a headline.
- EverArk Celebration of LIfe Software navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial
Write Biography
Write a biography for the memorial to commemorate one's life.
By writing a biography or an autobiography, you can leave an account of one's life to share with friends, family, and future generations.  It's a rewarding experience and an opportunity to step back and remember the good.  And, leaving behind life stories and memories can lift the spirits of those left behind.  
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Biography section
Add Obituary and Eulogy
Announce one's death in a meaningful and lasting way.
By writing an obituary and announcing one's death, you can share with family, friends, and the public one's passing. You can further honor their life by adding a eulogy to distribute electronically and/or read at the funeral service.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Obituary and Eulogy sections
Leave Messages
Leave messages and notes for your family and friends.
By leaving messages for your family and friends, you can be sure not to leave anything unsaid.  You can leave private messages, state your last wishes, and write a departing message for everyone.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Message sections
List Your Favorites
Add to your life story by sharing your favorite stories and memories.
By opening the company calendar, you can track all tasks, work orders, and meetings your team has scheduled for your entire cemetery.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Favorite sections
Publish & Share Memorial
Publish your memorial to the web to share with family and friends.
By publishing and sharing your online memorial, family and friends can share stories, memories, photos and more.  Your digital legacy will live on forever helping those you care about commemorate your memory and grieve.
- EverArk Celebration of Life navigation:  Digital Legacy > Memorial > Publish button
