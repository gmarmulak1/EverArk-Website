DO CEMETERIES NEED TO ADOPT TECHNOLOGY AND SOFTWARE?

Cemeteries may not necessarily need technology, but it can certainly enhance the experience and provide benefits to those who visit them.  Cemeteries can also benefit from the use of software to help manage and organize their operations. 
​​
What are some Cemetery Technology Considerations?

Cemetery Digital Mapping is Useful

One example of technology that could be useful in cemeteries is a digital mapping system that allows visitors to easily locate the graves of their loved ones. This can be especially helpful in larger cemeteries where it can be difficult to navigate.
​​

What About Virtual Cemetery Tours?

Another potential use for technology in cemeteries is to offer virtual tours or interactive displays that provide historical information about the cemetery and those buried there. This can help visitors gain a deeper understanding and appreciation for the site.
​​

Monitor the Condition of your Cemetery

Additionally, technology could be used to help preserve and maintain the cemetery, such as using drones to monitor the condition of the grounds or employing sensors to detect potential maintenance issues.
​​​
What are some Cemetery Software Considrations?

Cemetery Management Software

This type of software can help cemetery staff manage records of those buried, plot locations, maintenance schedules, and other important data.  A good cemetery management software acts an ERP system for your cemetery.  It should be easy to implement, intuitive to navigate, cloud-based, and affordable to use for a low monthly fee.

Cemetery Accounting Software

​This type of software can help manage financial transactions related to cemetery operations, such as sales of plots or services, and can also help track expenses related to maintenance and other operational costs.  For pre-need sales, having the ability to invoice a customer and provide financing services is also very important.
​

GIS Cemetery Software

​Geographical Information System (GIS) software can be used to create detailed maps of the cemetery grounds, including the location of graves, trees, pathways, and other features.  Allowing customers to view your cemetery gardens and plots so they can more easily select their place of burial will improve their experience.  A good cemetery management software will provide this for you.
​

Website and Social Media Software

Cemeteries can use software to manage their online presence, including their website and social media accounts. This can be used to provide information to the public, as well as to promote events and activities at the cemetery.  Using a cemetery management software that offers a full, online shopping experience to add to your website, like EverArk, is also important considering the shopping trends of today's consumer.

Overall, while technology may not be necessary for cemeteries to function, it can certainly provide benefits and enhance the visitor experience.  Similarly, cemetery software can certainly help improve efficiency and organization, and can provide a better experience for visitors.
