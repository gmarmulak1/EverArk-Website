What is a Cemetery CRM?
A Cemetery CRM is a customer relationship management system tailored for cemeteries to centralize contacts, manage roles and relationships, track interactions and activities, and link customers to plots and records for accurate service and sales.
How does EverArk link customers to plots and records?​
EverArk connects contact profiles to specific plots, graves, and records so teams can instantly view ownership, interment history, and open actions — reducing errors and saving time.
Does EverArk support reminders and activity tracking?
Yes. Staff can log calls, emails, and tasks; set reminders; and view timelines so nothing falls through the cracks during pre-need and at-need workflows.
