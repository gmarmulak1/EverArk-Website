DO CEMETERIES NEED REPORTING OR DASHBOARDS?

Yes, reporting and dashboards can be useful for cemeteries to track and analyze important data and metrics. Here are some reasons why:
​​

Track Burial and Plot Usage

A cemetery can use reporting and dashboards to track the number of burials, cremations, and other interments over time. This can help identify trends and forecast future demand for burial plots.  It also helps the manager keep an eye on the availability of plots so cemetery inventory does not get too low.
​​

Monitor Cemetery Revenue

Reporting and dashboards can help a cemetery track revenue and expenses, including income from burial fees and maintenance costs. A manager can quickly see where they may be losing money so they can focus their attention on the right problem area.  This can help ensure that the cemetery is operating efficiently and effectively.  
​​

Identify Cemetery Maintenance Needs

A cemetery can use reporting and dashboards to monitor the condition of burial plots, headstones, and other features.   A manager will be able to track the progress of work orders and the performance of the maintenance crew.  This can help identify areas that require maintenance or repairs, and prioritize these tasks based on their importance.
​​

Manage Cemetery Inventory

Reporting and dashboards can help a cemetery manage its inventory of burial plots, markers, and other materials.   A cemetery manage will be able to leverage the interactive maps to see the status of plots and the interments.  This can help ensure that the cemetery always has an adequate supply of these items on hand.

Overall, reporting and dashboards can provide valuable insights into a cemetery's operations and help ensure that it is meeting the needs of families and visitors. By tracking important data and metrics, a cemetery can make informed decisions and operate more efficiently and effectively.
