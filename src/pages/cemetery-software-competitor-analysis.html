UNLOCKING EFFICIENCY: A Comprehensive Competitor Analysis of the Best Cemetery Software for Managers

The 2025 Buyer’s Guide to Cemetery Management Software

How to Evaluate Today’s Top Platforms: PlotBox, Cemify, Chronicle, EverArk, CemSites, and Others
Choosing cemetery management software is a major decision—one that affects daily operations, long-term planning, public engagement, and the overall experience families have when navigating burial information. The market has grown quickly, and cemeteries now have access to tools once only available to large municipalities: cloud-based mapping, digital records, online storefronts, and even digital memorials.

This guide provides a neutral, research-driven comparison of leading solutions,including PlotBox, Cemify, Chronicle, EverArk, CemSites, and a few emerging providers. Rather than naming a “winner,” it highlights what each platform does well and where they differ, so cemeteries can find the best fit for their unique operations.
​

Understanding the Landscape: Categories of Cemetery Software
Before comparing vendors, it helps to understand the three dominant categories of cemetery systems in today’s market:

1. Operational Management Suites
These focus on day-to-day cemetery administration—interment tracking, inventory control, work orders, financials, and compliance.  PlotBox and CemSites tend to fall here, offering comprehensive operational modules designed for larger or more complex organizations.

2. Mapping & Digitization Platforms
These emphasize GIS mapping, public cemetery search, and converting old paper maps into modern, interactive records. Chronicle, Cemify, and newer free-to-start systems, EverARk, often focus on mapping and basic record management.

3. Modern “Hybrid” Platforms
A newer category that blends operations, CRM, mapping, record management, ecommerce, and digital memorial experiences into one system. EverArk represents this trend—combining management tools with online storefronts and digital legacy features.

Understanding where each solution sits helps frame what to expect as you evaluate options.
​

Comparing the Leading Cemetery Software Solutions

Chronicle
Chronicle focuses strongly on mapping, public search, and clean online visuals. Councils and regional cemeteries often choose it because of its emphasis on public-facing tools like walking directions and plot lookups. Cemeteries seeking modern maps but minimal operational overhead tend to appreciate its clarity.

​Cemify
Cemify appeals primarily to small and mid-sized cemeteries seeking a clean, easy-to-use system. It digitizes paper maps, supports standard recordkeeping, and offers a cost-effective entry into cemetery software. Its simplicity is its primary value, making it ideal for church-run cemeteries or volunteer-managed grounds.

CemSites
CemSites offers a modular approach—cemeteries can adopt CRM, finance, mapping, or other components as needed. It appeals to organizations that also manage funeral homes or crematories and want a unified “death-care” system. While powerful, it is often configured through add-on modules, which may require more planning for smaller cemeteries.

​PlotBox
PlotBox is widely known as a full enterprise platform used by large cemeteries, councils, and multi-location organizations. Its strengths include GIS-based mapping, operational depth, and a professional implementation approach. For complex operations with numerous staff, PlotBox provides structure, controls, and robust reporting.

EverArk
EverArk is one of the newer platforms and reflects a shift toward modern, integrated all-in-one cemetery software. It includes features typically spread across multiple vendors—interactive mapping, record management, operations, interment management, CRM, dashboards, digital signatures, an online cemetery storefront, and digital memorials—all inside one cloud-based environment.

What stands out is its focus on the family experience as much as internal operations. In addition to standard management tools, EverArk includes:
  • A built-in online storefront to sell plots or services
  • Digital memorial pages (“Digitaphs”), family trees, and celebration-of-life content
  • Modern dashboards and workspaces
  • Cloud mapping conversions from existing paper maps
​
For cemeteries seeking both operational efficiency and new digital engagement opportunities, EverArk represents where the industry is heading.

Feature-by-Feature Evaluation: What Buyers Should Look For

1. Mapping & Records Digitization
All major vendors now offer digital mapping, but approaches vary significantly. PlotBox offers drone-validated GIS mapping; Chronicle emphasizes public access and visuals; Cemify and EverArk focus on converting existing paper maps into modern interactive systems. EverArk's modern approach of using Google Maps also enables integrations to Google Earth which reduces implementation costs significantly.

2. Plot Inventory & Ownership Tracking
This is foundational. All platforms support inventory and ownership, though more advanced options—such as reservation tracking, price lists, and revenue dashboards—may require additional modules with some vendors.

3. Work Orders, Tasks & Operations
Larger systems like PlotBox and CemSites offer operational features. Chronicle and Cemify support simpler workflows. EverArk includes task management and work orders, blending ease of use with enough structure for cemeteries with dedicated staff to efficiently manage approvals and inspections.

4. Online Public Search & Family Engagement
PlotBox’s EverAfter, Chronicle’s public search, Cemify’s online maps, and EverArk’s public Digitaphs all serve this need. The biggest differences lie in how interactive and modern the experiences feel.

5. Digital Memorials & Legacy Content
This has become a differentiator. Memorials used to live on separate websites; now they’re being integrated into cemetery platforms. EverArk provides the most developed version of this, with family trees, celebration-of-life content, and digital memorials built directly into the system.  Cemeteries can leverage EverArk to sell Celebration of Life tools and package to families to make the passing of a loved one more meaningful.

6. Ecommerce for Plot Sales & Services
This is where the market is changing quickly. Only a few platforms offer native ecommerce, while others rely on integrations or third-party websites. EverArk includes a built-in storefront, and CemSites offers ecommerce through select modules. For cemeteries looking to modernize revenue channels, this capability is increasingly important.

Which System Is the Best Fit?

Because cemeteries vary widely in size, staff, budget, and goals, no one platform is perfect for everyone. But some patterns emerge:
  • Large or multi-location cemeteries often prefer PlotBox or CemSites for operational structure.  EverArk allows for multiple cemeteries but as newer player in the space, doesn't have as many references.
  • Small to mid-size cemeteries lean toward Cemify, EverArk, or Chronicle for ease of use.
  • Cemeteries wanting modern tools like ecommerce and digital memorials tend to look toward newer platforms like EverArk.
  • Cemeteries wanting cheaper implementations tend to lean towards EverArk with its online setup tools and training videos for a cemetery to self-implement the product at their own pace.
  • Municipal or council-operated cemeteries frequently choose Chronicle for its public-mapping strengths.
  • Hybrid organizations (cemeteries + funeral homes) may find CemSites the most modular.

In recent years, many of the newest cemeteries seeking a more modern, family-friendly digital presence have shown growing interest in integrated platforms that combine operations, mapping, memorials, and online sales—an area where EverArk’s feature mix aligns naturally.
​

Final Thoughts: Choosing with Confidence

The key to choosing cemetery software is not the size of the feature list but how well the system supports your cemetery’s operational realities and the digital expectations of today’s families.
​
As you compare vendors, consider:
  • How much staff time you want to save
  • How modern you want your public-facing presence to be
  • Whether you intend to sell plots or services online
  • How important digital memorials or legacy tools are to your community
  • How comfortable your team is with complex vs. simple software
  • Whether you want one platform or a combination of tools

Most cemeteries today are looking for more than just digital records—they want tools that support modern engagement, streamline operations, and help families connect more deeply with the memories of their loved ones.

Platforms like PlotBox, Cemify, Chronicle, and CemSites all offer meaningful capabilities depending on your needs. Emerging hybrid systems like EverArk illustrate where the industry is naturally evolving: toward unified platforms that make operations easier while enhancing the family’s digital experience.
