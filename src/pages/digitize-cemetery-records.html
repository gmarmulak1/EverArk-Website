WHY SHOULD CEMETERIES DIGITIZE THEIR RECORDS?

Cemeteries have been storing and saving hardcopies of deeds and sales contracts for hundreds of years.  Why change something when it is not broken.  ​​There are several reasons why cemeteries should consider digitizing their records:
​

Quickly Access Your Cemetery Records

Digitizing cemetery records makes them easily accessible to the public. People can search for their loved ones' burial sites and other details from the comfort of their own homes. This can save them time and effort, and also make it easier for them to plan visits to the cemetery.
​​​

Preserve Your Cemetery Files

Digitizing records also helps to preserve them. Paper records are vulnerable to damage from water, fire, pests, and other factors. By digitizing them, cemeteries can ensure that their records are safely stored and protected for future generations.  This is especially important for cemeteries that have strict restrictions and annual licensing reviews, like in California.
​​

Make It Easier for Your Cemetery Administration

Digitizing cemetery files can also make cemetery operations more efficient. Staff can quickly and easily access information, update records, and perform other tasks without having to sift through paper files.  One of the most time-consuming job of your cemetery administration is to find and locate records.  Why not give them an easier way to do their job?
​​​​

Save Your Cemetery Money

Digitizing cemetery records can also save money in the long run. It eliminates the need for paper, ink, and other supplies used in maintaining paper records. It can also reduce the need for staff to maintain physical records, freeing them up to focus on other tasks.
​

Reduce Manual Errors

Digitizing records can also improve accuracy. By using digital tools to manage records, staff can ensure that information is entered accurately and consistently, reducing the risk of errors.  Cemetery staff will be less stressed about constantly reviewing and editing cemetery sales contracts.

Overall, digitizing cemetery records can provide many benefits, including increased accessibility, preservation, efficiency, cost savings, and accuracy.  Implementing a cemetery management software like EverArk eliminates the risk of tracking manual records and improves the quality of life for your cemetery administration.
