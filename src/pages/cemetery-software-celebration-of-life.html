CEMETERY MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE | Celebration of Life App

Give your customers an app to share their story

EverArk's cemetery celebration of life app is a digital legacy experience built for your cemetery customers. Your customers can add an online memorial, funeral notice, family tree, resting place, and much more. Let them leave a digital footprint to share with family and friends that will live on.

See how Celebration of Life works.
Sell Packages
Earn money for your cemetery by selling Celebration of Life software packages.
By selling Celebration of Life packages, your customers will be able to create an online digital legacy using EverArk.  Customers can purchase from your cemetery a Single package, a Family package, or a Family and Friends Package.  To learn more, check out our Pricing page.
Sign Up Online
Once a package is purchased, your customer can sign up online.
By a purchasing a celebration of life package, the purchaser will get an email to register and sign up online.  Once their account is created, the user can start building digital legacies for themselves and/or for a loved one.
Start Celebration of Life
Customers can build their own digital legacy or one for someone else.
By signing into the celebration of life website, the user can either start creating a digital legacy for themselves, or for someone else.  The digital legacy wizard helps users create a dedicated memorial page which includes pictures, tributes, a biography, an obituary, a eulogy, special moments, a farewell post, private messages, and much more.
Publish Memorial
When a memorial is ready, it can be published it to the web.
By completing the memorial and/or funeral notice section of the digital legacy, the user can publish it to the web.  Users can control who has access to see the memorial and/or funeral notice by uploading contacts and managing their privacy settings.
Share Digital Legacy
Customers can share their digital legacy with family and friends .
By publishing the memorial and/or funeral notice of the digital legacy, the user can share the public URL with friends and family.  If the URL is public, it is also searchable on the EverArk App webpage.
Download App
Share the celebration of life app with friends and family to download.
By sharing the EverArk celebration of life app, friends and family can download the app and access their published digital legacy.  In the app, friends and family can share photos, videos, tributes, and more.  ​
