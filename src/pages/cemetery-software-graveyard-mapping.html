HOW DOES EVERARK'S MAPPING WORK? A STEP-BY-STEP FEATURE TOUR.

EverArk’s cemetery mapping software makes it easy to create an accurate, interactive graveyard map. Click on plots to see details, filter by availability, and give families and staff instant access to the information they need.

Graveyard Mapping

Map your entire cemetery to easily see plots, boundaries, and paths.
Map your cemetery sections and boundaries.
By opening up your online, interactive cemetery maps, you get a satellite view of your entire cemetery where you can zoom in and out.  You can easily see and filter plots by status and interment.

Interactive Maps

Zoom in, zoom out, or click on a plot in your cemetery map to see all its details and attributes.
With EverArk, every plot is displayed in an interactive map view. Zoom in to specific sections, locate graves instantly, and access ownership or burial details with a single click. Cemetery managers save time, reduce errors, and eliminate the guesswork that comes with paper maps.​
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Inventory > Map View  | Action Menu > Show Map

Configure View & Filters

Design your perfect inventory plot view to better manage your plots.
By configuring your cemetery Inventory List View, you can create the company default view by reordering columns, adding/removing fields, applying custom filters, and much more.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Reports > Inventory > Set Inventory View

Add New Plot Fields

Add custom plot fields to your inventory view to personalize your experience.
Customize plots attributes.
By adding new custom plot fields, you can track plot data that is specific to your cemetery.  You can also create views and filters using those fields to improve your navigation experience.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Settings > Properties > Plots tab  | Reports > Inventory > Set Inventory View
Manage plot details with bulk edits.

Mass Update Plots

Make and save updates to large amounts of inventory plot data.
By using the Inventory Update Plot(s) option, you can change or update fields for multiple plots at once via bulk edit.  You can manually select the plots you want to update, filter for a subset of plots, or update all plots.  Similarly, you can delete multiple plots at once.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Inventory > Update Plot(s)

Frequently Asked Questions | CEMETERY MAPPING

Can't find what you're looking for?  We would love to chat with you.
What do you need for EverArk's cemetery maps?
EverArk's cemetery maps are built on Google maps.  To display sections, plots, and boundaries you only need the centroid GPS coordinates of each plot.
How do I get the GPS coordinates for my plots?
There are several ways to get GPS coordinates for the plots in your cemetery if you don't already have them.  The most accurate, and sometimes the most expensive, way is to have a surveyor come to your cemetery and capture the GPS coordinates of each plot.  Alternatively, the cheapest and fastest way is to have ChatGPT calculate the GPS coordinates of your plots.  For more info on how to do this, you can read this blog post - ChatGPT Instructions to Map your Cemetery for EverArk.
How do I setup the cemetery maps?
The easiest way is to use EveArk's excel-based, plot import template that you can download within the cemetery software.  Simply follow the layout and formatting of the plot worksheet to import your plot and GPS data.
Who can see the cemetery maps?
Your users will have access to your cemetery maps unless you update their role to not include it.  Customers can also access your cemetery map to see the available plots via your online, ecommerce website powered by EverArk.
How do I know which plots are sold?  Interred?
You have quick filters that allow you to see Sold, Available, Pending, Hold, and/or Available plots, along with plots that are Interred or Not Interred.  Similarly, the statuses of each plot are color-coded so you can identify them visually.
