What are the Challenges of Managing Cemetery Operations?

Most cemeteries still rely on spreadsheets or paper logs to manage daily operations. This often leads to:
  • Missed maintenance tasks or delayed service orders
  • Lack of visibility into what the grounds crew is working on
  • Difficulty coordinating burial services and work schedules
  • No historical record of past work or service timelines
  • Communication gaps between office staff and field teams
  • Inefficient, manual follow-up for overdue or urgent tasks

What are some common use cases of managing cemetery operations?

Cemetery operations teams, like grounds crew and office staff, use EverArk to streamline:
  • Grounds maintenance (mowing, trimming, landscaping)
  • Marker installation and replacement tasks
  • Burial preparation and service setup
  • Equipment maintenance schedules
  • Seasonal or recurring clean-up projects
  • Customer service requests
  • Interment-related work orders
EverArk solves these problems and addresses these use cases by giving cemeteries a unified, easy-to-use platform built specifically for operational efficiency.
