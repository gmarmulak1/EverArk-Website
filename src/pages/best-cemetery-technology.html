WHAT ARE SOME OF THE BEST CEMETERY TECH COMPANIES?

Technology companies focused on the cemetery industry are leveraging digital tools to improve cemetery management, enhance customer experience, and streamline operations. Here are some of the leading tech companies that provide valuable services to cemeteries:​
EverArk
  • Description: EverArk is designed to support cemetery management and operations with intuitive tools for digital mapping, record-keeping, and plot sales. It aims to make cemetery management simpler and more efficient for both operators and customers, with an emphasis on user-friendly interfaces and a holistic view of cemetery data.
  • Key Features:
    • Cloud-Based Platform: Accessible from anywhere, allowing cemetery staff to manage operations remotely.
    • Plot Mapping and Visualization: Interactive maps that make it easy for staff and customers to view available plots, manage reservations, and locate specific gravesites.
    • Record Management: Digital record storage, which helps streamline access to historical records and keeps data centralized.
    • Customer Relationship Management (CRM): Tools for managing interactions with customers, handling sales inquiries, and tracking customer preferences.
    • Memorialization Options: Options for creating online memorials and virtual tours, enhancing the experience for families.
    • E-commerce Capabilities: Supports plot reservations and sales, helping cemeteries manage pre-need and at-need purchases more effectively.

​EverArk’s modern approach makes it a compelling choice for cemeteries that want a robust, all-in-one platform without a steep learning curve. Its cloud-based nature also offers a lot of flexibility, making it suitable for cemeteries looking to transition their management to a digital-first approach.

Afterword
  • Description: Afterword is a digital memorialization platform that allows cemeteries to offer families virtual memorial spaces. It focuses on remembrance and legacy management.
  • Key Features:
    • Online memorial pages with customizable content
    • Tools for family members to add memories and photos
    • Digital guestbooks and messaging
    • Integrations with cemetery management software

Chronicle
  • Description: Chronicle focuses on cemetery data management and plot visualization through mapping tools. Its main purpose is to create an accurate digital map of burial plots, along with comprehensive data storage and retrieval.
  • Key Features:
    • Detailed, interactive cemetery maps for plot management
    • Data digitization for easy access to historical records
    • Mobile accessibility for on-the-go data entry
    • Geolocation capabilities for finding and identifying plots
​
Cemetery360
  • Description: Cemetery360 specializes in creating virtual cemetery tours and online plot visualization. This is especially valuable for cemeteries that want to offer virtual visits or plot reservations.
  • Key Features:
    • 360-degree virtual tours of cemetery grounds
    • Interactive plot mapping for easy navigation
    • Visual tools for pre-need and at-need plot sales
    • Customizable interfaces to match the look and feel of the cemetery’s website

WebCemeteries
  • Description: WebCemeteries provides digital tools and services to cemeteries, focusing on record digitization, mapping, and online memorialization. It helps cemeteries improve customer experience by offering easy online access to records and burial information.
  • Key Features:
    • Online plot management and mapping tools
    • Record digitization and data management
    • Tools for creating and maintaining online memorials
    • Family-friendly interfaces to allow self-service access

Qeepr
  • Description: Qeepr is a platform that focuses on online memorialization and social media-style pages for families. It allows cemeteries to offer families options to create interactive memorials that can be visited virtually.
  • Key Features:
    • Online memorials with photos, videos, and stories
    • Integration with cemetery websites
    • Visitor engagement tools, such as digital guestbooks
    • Virtual candles and other digital tribute options

Grave Discover
  • Description: Grave Discover is a cemetery management software that focuses on simplifying digital record management and plot mapping, making it easier for staff to locate, manage, and visualize cemetery data.
  • Key Features:
    • GPS-enabled mapping for easy plot location
    • Record digitization for quick data retrieval
    • Public search options for burial records
    • User-friendly interface for staff and public use

Eternalspace
  • Description: Eternalspace is a memorialization platform that allows cemeteries and families to create virtual memorials with photos, videos, and stories. It focuses on enhancing remembrance through digital means.
  • Key Features:
    • Interactive online memorials for loved ones
    • Digital tributes and family engagement tools
    • Options for creating a legacy page that friends and family can visit
    • Integrations with social media platforms for sharing memorials


​These companies help cemeteries modernize operations, improve customer interaction, and preserve data digitally.
