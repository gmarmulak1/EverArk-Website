BRINGING SOFTWARE AND NEW IDEAS TO CEMETERIES

Our software was originally developed to support our own green burial sites.  Our passion is to restore, protect and preserve nature through the endowments provided by those laid to rest on our properties.  As no other software could support all our needs from burial management, eCommerce, GPS plot assignments, and online, social memorials, etc., we developed a state-of-the-art software which is now available to you.  So, what started as a passion is now our mission.
