Why is Cemetery Order Management Software Important for Cemeteries?

Managing cemetery orders is more than just tracking sales—it’s ensuring families receive timely and accurate service during emotionally sensitive times. Manual processes leave room for errors, delayed payments, and lost paperwork, which can impact both your operations and customer relationships. By centralizing orders, invoices, and communications in one system, cemeteries gain full visibility into every transaction from quote to payment.

Email quotes for approval.

Accept Credit Card Payments
Create drafts and send invoices for online payment by credit card.
Accept credit card payments.
By creating a new invoice, you can save a draft for review.  When ready, you can email the invoice to a customer for online payment by leveraging our Stripe or PayPal integration.  You can also track the status of your invoices - Declined, Late, Partial, and Paid.
- EverArk Cemetery Software navigation:  Cemetery > Invoices > Review or Edit  | Action Menu > New Invoice
