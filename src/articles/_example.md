## What the front matter does

`title`, `date` and `description` are required. Everything else is optional:

- `keywords` — the meta keywords for this page
- `image` — a repository-relative path, used for the social card
- `tags` — free-form, for grouping later
- `banner` / `bannerImage` — override the hero if this post needs its own
- `seoTitle` — override the `<title>` when it should differ from the headline
- `draft` — keep it out of the build

## Writing

Ordinary Markdown. Headings, **bold**, [links](cemetery-software-features.html),
lists, quotes and images all work:

> The styling comes from `assets/css/article.css`, which is about 40 lines.
> The posts carried over from Weebly inline roughly 8 KB of font declarations
> per heading instead.
